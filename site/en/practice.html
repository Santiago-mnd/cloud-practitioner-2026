Practice

The 240 original CLF‑C02 questions are still being written. They follow the weight of each exam domain: 58 for Cloud Concepts, 72 for Security and Compliance, 81 for Cloud Technology and Services, and 29 for Billing, Pricing, and Support.

Once they’re ready, you’ll be able to start with a 20-question assessment, followed by spaced review of what you miss and a 65-question practice exam in 90 minutes, like the real exam.
